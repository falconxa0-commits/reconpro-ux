  1278→  1278→  1630→        """Open finding detail modal for the clicked finding.
  1279→  1279→  1631→
  1280→  1280→  1632→        Uses the vertical click position to determine which finding index

  1301→  1301→  1653→                        line_height = event.widget._line_height
  1302→  1302→  1654→                    clicked_index = int((click_y + scroll_offset * line_height) / line_height)
