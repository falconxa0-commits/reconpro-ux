    GORGON ULTRA is the crown jewel of the Scan Engine. It runs fifteen sequential analysis stages against a target — and I want to stress that word sequential, because each stage's output feeds into the next. It starts with basic fingerprinting and technology detection, moves through port scanning and TLS auditing, then dives into DNS reconnaissance, SSL verification, cookie and header analysis. After that, it probes for information leaks and known vulnerabilities, runs active XSS, SQL injection, and remote code execution tests, and finishes with a configuration audit. All fifteen stages converge into what we call the Fear Index. The Fear Index takes all of those disparate findings and synthesizes them into a single threat score with five levels: Subtle, Notable, Alarming, Devastating, and Omnipotent. No other open-source tool provides this kind of unified threat quantification. You can look at one number and instantly understand how badly a target is exposed. That's the power of GORGON.
