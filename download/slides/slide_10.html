All integrations use standard protocols. No proprietary agents needed.
ReconPro doesn't ask you to rip out your existing security stack. It plugs into the tools your team already uses. We support six enterprise integrations out of the box. Jira integration automatically creates tickets from scan findings — every critical vulnerability becomes a tracked issue with full context. Slack sends real-time alerts to your security channels as scans complete, so your team knows immediately when something lands. GitHub integration pushes security reports directly into your repositories, perfect for dev teams who want findings in their pull request workflow. Splunk forwards all findings to your SIEM via HEC or REST, so your SOC analysts can correlate ReconPro data with everything else. PagerDuty triggers incident escalation for critical findings, making sure the right people get paged. And Z.AI uses Server-Sent Events for real-time AI-powered analysis of your scan results as they stream in. The key point: every integration uses standard, well-documented protocols. There's no proprietary agent to install, no reverse proxy to configure. You just point and go.
