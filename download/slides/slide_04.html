THE SCAN ENGINE

Three flagship modules. Zero mercy for threats.

Now we're going to dive deep into the core of ReconPro — the Scan Engine. This is where the heavy lifting happens. Over the next few slides, we're going to walk through three flagship modules that set ReconPro apart from every other open-source security tool out there. First, GORGON ULTRA — a fifteen-stage threat analysis engine that produces something called the Fear Index. Second, OBLIVION — a twenty-stage deep vulnerability scanner with its own DREAD scoring system and a severity level that goes above critical. And third, PEGASUS HUNTER — a module specifically designed to detect the world's most sophisticated spyware, including indicators from Pegasus and other nation-state malware. These three modules represent the cutting edge of what's possible in open-source security tooling. Let's start with GORGON.
