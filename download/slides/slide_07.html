PEGASUS HUNTER is our third flagship module, and it tackles a problem most open-source tools completely ignore: nation-state spyware detection. We've compiled a curated threat intelligence database of 135 known command-and-control domains associated with Pegasus, Hermit Worm, and other sophisticated surveillance malware used by governments worldwide. The module checks 11 SMS-based attack delivery patterns, 19 suspicious process signatures that indicate compromise, and 9 filesystem path indicators where these tools typically store their payloads. This isn't theoretical — these are real threat indicators used in documented attacks against journalists, activists, and political figures. If you're a security researcher, a defender, or someone working in digital rights, this module gives you the ability to scan infrastructure and endpoints for traces of the world's most dangerous spyware, all from a single CLI command.
