OBLIVION is ReconPro's deep vulnerability scanner, and it's a beast. While GORGON focuses on threat assessment and synthesis, OBLIVION goes deep — twenty analysis stages that include SQL injection probing, cross-site scripting hunting, server-side request forgery chain exploitation, path traversal detection, and much more. Every single finding is scored on the DREAD scale: Info, Low, Medium, High, Critical. But here's what makes OBLIVION unique — it introduces a sixth severity level called TRANSCENDENT. This is a level above critical, reserved for vulnerabilities that aren't just severe in isolation but that chain together to create existential threats. Only OBLIVION uses this level. Beyond scoring, OBLIVION has two features you won't find anywhere else. Wisdom Verdict is an AI-powered summary that takes all twenty stages of output and gives you a plain-English assessment of the target's security posture. Mirror Fracture decomposes the attack surface and shows you exactly how individual vulnerabilities chain together into a complete kill chain. These two features alone justify the entire platform.
